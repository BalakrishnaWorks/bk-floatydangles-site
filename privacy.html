Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the BK FLOATYDANGLES desktop application and visit the official BK FLOATYDANGLES website.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. About BK FLOATYDANGLES

BK FLOATYDANGLES is a desktop application project that provides floating desktop charms for Windows. Its website provides product information, downloads, updates and a feedback facility.

The desktop application is offered free of charge. There is no paid subscription required to use the currently offered application.

2. Desktop Application

The desktop application is designed to provide its desktop charm experience on Windows computers. Some information may be processed locally by the application to provide its features and remember application settings.

The application may interact with the Windows operating system and files or settings required for its normal operation. The exact information accessed depends on the features being used.

This policy does not claim that the application collects absolutely no information or makes no network connections. Its complete internal data handling has not been independently audited for this policy.

Users should download the application only from the official BK FLOATYDANGLES distribution page.

3. Website Information

When visiting the website, your browser and hosting infrastructure may process technical information necessary to deliver pages, maintain availability and protect the service.

The website may also use Vercel Analytics to understand general visitor activity and website performance, once analytics is enabled.

4. Feedback Form

Visitors can voluntarily submit suggestions, bug reports and other feedback through the website.

The feedback form may collect:

  • The feedback message you submit.
  • The date and time the submission is recorded.
  • An email address, if you voluntarily provide one.

Email addresses are optional. They may be used by the project owner to respond to a feedback submission. Submitting an email address does not subscribe you to marketing emails or create an account.

5. Firebase and Data Storage

The website uses Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to store feedback submissions.

Feedback is not publicly displayed on the website. Public visitors are not given permission through the website's Firestore rules to read, edit or delete stored feedback.

Firebase is a third-party service and processes information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

6. Website Analytics

Vercel Analytics may be used to understand general website traffic, page visits and performance. Analytics configuration and data handling are subject to Vercel's policies.

Analytics is separate from the feedback form. This policy does not promise that analytics collects no technical or visitor information.

7. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Review suggestions and reported problems.
  • Improve BK FLOATYDANGLES.
  • Respond to feedback when an email address is supplied.
  • Understand website performance and usage.
  • Protect the services from misuse and technical problems.

8. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the website and its features, including Firebase and Vercel.

Feedback is not intentionally published or sold. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

9. Security

Reasonable measures are used to configure and maintain the website and its feedback database. Access restrictions are applied to stored feedback.

However, no website, application, database or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should avoid including passwords, payment details, sensitive personal information or information about other people in feedback.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Feedback may be retained while it remains useful for reviewing suggestions, resolving issues and improving the project.

You may request deletion of a feedback submission by contacting the project owner. Requests will be reviewed and handled where reasonably possible and legally appropriate.

11. Children's Privacy

The website and application are not intended to collect unnecessary personal information from children. Please do not submit sensitive personal information or another person's private information through the feedback form.

12. Third-Party Services

The project may rely on third-party services such as:

  • Google Firebase — feedback database services.
  • Vercel — website hosting and analytics.
  • GitHub — application source and release distribution.

These services have their own terms and privacy policies. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, website or services change. The latest version will be published on this page.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or feedback deletion requests, please use the official contact method published on the BK FLOATYDANGLES website.
